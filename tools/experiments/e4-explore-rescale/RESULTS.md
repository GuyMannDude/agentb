## Reading the table

- `offset` up ⇒ `diff` up and `prec` down, almost monotonically: the further
  the target sits from the bullseye the more explore-only finds, and the
  more noise. The rule's precision floor is what stops the slide.
- `floor` 0.6 is too tight everywhere (diff ≤ 0.25); 1.0 lets the tail in.
- `scale` matters least; 0.30 is the narrowest band that does not starve.
- A similarity-only lens (see the harness control) finds adjacent items as
  well as the pick; the pick's edge is divergence + one extra explore-only
  find over 16 queries. Modest, real, instrumented.
